Fotoreto smile line model ("Làm mờ rãnh cười AI") — vendor/skin-ai/ranh-cuoi-v1.onnx

Own model of Fotoreto / Hoàng Phúc Photo (Nguyễn Hoàng Phúc), trained 03/11/2026 from scratch on
the author's own before / after smile line retouch photo pairs with tools/train-da (train.py: U-Net, ~2.7 million parameters,
Conv / ReLU / max-pool / bilinear upsampling only). No third-party weights, code or datasets are included.
Weights stored as float16 (tools/onnx-fp16-store.py), computed in float32.
