DEVSTU skin retouch model ("Làm đẹp da AI") — vendor/skin-ai/da-retouch-v1.onnx

Own model of DEVSTU / Hoàng Phúc Photo (Nguyễn Hoàng Phúc), trained 31/10/2026 from scratch on the author's own
before / after skin-retouch photo pairs (~5,400 photos, ~7,100 faces) with tools/train-da (train.py: U-Net, ~2.7 million
parameters, Conv / ReLU / max-pool / bilinear upsampling only). No third-party weights, code or datasets are included.
Weights stored as float16 (tools/onnx-fp16-store.py), computed in float32.
