Fotoreto Body skin retouch model ("Làm đẹp da cơ thể AI") — vendor/skin-ai/da-than-v1.onnx

Own model of Fotoreto / Hoàng Phúc Photo (Nguyễn Hoàng Phúc), trained 02/11/2026 from scratch on
the author's own before / after body-skin retouch photo pairs (about 3,000 photos) with tools/train-da (train.py: U-Net, ~2.7 million parameters,
Conv / ReLU / max-pool / bilinear upsampling only). No third-party weights, code or datasets are included.
Weights stored as float16 (tools/onnx-fp16-store.py), computed in float32.
