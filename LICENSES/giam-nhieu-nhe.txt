Fotoreto light AI noise reduction model ("Giảm nhiễu bằng AI") — vendor/giam-nhieu-nhe/giam-nhieu-nhe-v1.enc (float32) + giam-nhieu-nhe-v1-f16.enc (float16)

Own model of Fotoreto / Hoàng Phúc Photo (Nguyễn Hoàng Phúc), trained 06/11/2026: U-Net with only 3x3 convolutions, ReLU, strided-conv
downsampling and pixel-shuffle upsampling, ~4.2 million parameters. Trained by distillation: the targets are outputs of SCUNet
(Apache License 2.0 — see scunet.txt) on ~11,500 crops of the author's own RAW / JPEG photos (1,920 files). No SCUNet weights or code
are included in this model. Stored encrypted (AES-256-GCM); the key is given by the Fotoreto server to Premium users.
